*****************************
Showcase editorial guidelines
*****************************

The Showcase is a quarterly, editor-picked selection of content submitted by the Write the Docs community, so people don't have to dig for quality articles across multiple channels or scroll endlessly through their newsfeed.
Not everything submitted gets featured, and that's the point. Of course, the `#community-showcase <https://writethedocs.slack.com/archives/CLMSZGGK1>`__ Slack channel remains open to anyone, anytime. You are always welcome to post there as well.

In the Showcase, we don't republish the full text. We link out to each featured piece and give a short write-up explaining why it's worth reading.

What we're looking for
**********************

* **Relevance.** Content must be related to the practice of writing, creating, maintaining, or building documentation; or related to the discipline of technical writing. It shouldn't be primarily a product marketing piece. If the main point is "buy this" or "use this tool," it's not a fit, regardless of how well-written it is.

* **Quality.**  Submissions should be readable and clear — writing that respects the reader. Beyond that, we're not grading prose style. A first blog post that says something real will always beat a polished piece that doesn't.

* **Alignment.** Content should align with Write the Docs' :doc:`Guiding Principles </about-us>` of openness, inclusivity, and community. If in doubt, leave it out.

* **Freshness.** Content should have been published within the previous three months.

* **Public accessibility.** Content must be freely readable — not behind a paywall, login, or gift link.

* **Process over tooling.** As with our conference talks, we're wary of pieces that are really just tutorials or pitches for a specific tool. We'd rather feature something about process, culture, people, or the harder problems behind documentation work. Tooling pieces are okay, but they need to bring something more than "here's how to use X."

* **Practicality and a point of view.** We lean toward content backed by real experience over pure theory, and toward pieces that take a clear position rather than staying neutral for its own sake.

Who can submit
**************

The person submitting must be a Write the Docs community member. We ask for a Slack handle as your credential to confirm this. If you nominate someone else's work, they don't have to be a community member themselves.

Quality, not pedigree
*********************

We welcome submissions from people at every stage of their career — first-time bloggers and twenty-year veterans alike. "Quality" means the piece is clear, genuine, and has something real to say. A first attempt at writing about a documentation problem you just solved can easily outshine a slicker piece that doesn't say much. We're not grading craft for its own sake - we're looking for content that's useful or interesting to the community, regardless of who wrote it or how long they've been doing this.

If you're not sure whether your work is "good enough," that's probably not a reason to hold back. Submit it anyway.

Content written for an employer
*******************************

Content written as part of someone's job (a company blog post, for instance) is eligible, but personal, independently-written content is prioritised. This is about keeping the Showcase's vibe one of individual community members sharing their own work and thinking, rather than becoming another company-blog distribution channel.

What we accept, for now
***********************

For the first editions of the Showcase, we're only accepting **written content** like articles and blog posts. We may expand this to podcasts, videos, and projects, but each of those brings its own evaluation effort, so we're starting narrow and may widen scope once the process is running smoothly.

How often the same person can appear
************************************

To keep the Showcase reflecting a range of voices rather than a few favourites, we cap how often the same content creator appears: **twice per calendar year**, maximum.

A note on endorsement
*********************

Being featured in the Showcase means our editorial team read it and thought it was worth sharing. It isn't a sponsorship, and Write the Docs isn't vouching for every claim or opinion in a piece.
