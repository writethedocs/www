Community showcase
==================

The Write the Docs community produces a lot of great writing, and our Showcase is a way to help it find the audience it deserves.

Every quarter, we feature articles submitted by members of the Write the Docs community, surfaced for an audience of over 25,000+ documentarians in our `Slack network <https://www.writethedocs.org/slack/>`__.

If you care about documentation and you've written something great or read something that made you stop and think, we want to hear about it.

What we're looking for
----------------------

Blog posts or articles about software documentation and related topics.

It should be:

* **Public** — readable without a login and not behind a paywall. Gift links expire, so we don't accept those either.
* **Recent** — published in the last three months
* **Not an ad** — if it's mainly a pitch for a product, it's not a fit
* **Yours, or someone else's** — nominate your own work, or someone you think deserves the spotlight

See the full :doc:`editorial guidelines </organizer-guide/showcase/editorial-guidelines>` for the complete rules, including how we handle employer-written content.

Not every submission gets featured. We read everything and pick what we think is most worth sharing.
