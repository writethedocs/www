Fishbowl: Change my mind
==============================

**It's time for your spiciest docs opinion, the one you've never said out loud.** This year, Write the Docs Australia is trying something new: a fishbowl session where hot takes get debated live on stage, and anyone can jump in.

The fishbowl runs on Day 1 at 14:00, straight after the lightning talks.

What's a fishbowl?
------------------------------

A fishbowl is a discussion with a twist. Five chairs sit in a semicircle on stage: one for our host, three for speakers, and one that's always empty.

The empty chair is to remind us to keep an open mind, it represents the viewpoint we haven't heard yet. If you want to join the conversation, come up and sit in it. But when you do, someone on stage has to vacate their chair to make room for the new fish, and the conversation keeps flowing.

Everyone else gets to look in on some lively debate. And no, it's not Thunderdome. Everyone gets to leave.

Why we're trying it
------------------------------

Write the Docs Australia has always been a risk taker. We like trying new things, and some of our experiments have gone on to be adopted by other Write the Docs events around the world.

The fishbowl is our latest experiment. It gives the kind of conversations you usually have in the hallway a bit of structure, while leaving plenty of room for the unexpected. We think it's a fun way to get candid, hear different viewpoints, and maybe even change a mind or two.

How it works
------------------------------

- **Drop in a hot take**: From the moment doors open on Day 1, you'll find a fishbowl in the gathering area. Write a bold statement on a slip and drop it in. The bowl closes at lunch.
- **We pick the topics**: Over lunch, the team reads every slip and picks the takes that came up most. Our host reads out the topics at the start of the session.
- **Pick your row**: The first three rows of seats are the waiting room, one row per topic. If you'd like to speak, sit in the row for your topic. If you'd rather watch, sit anywhere in the theatre.
- **Take the empty chair**: When you're ready, walk up on stage and sit in the empty chair. Our host will bring you into the conversation.
- **Card and bell**: A friendly timekeeper keeps things moving, so lots of people get a turn. When you see the "Final thought?" card, start wrapping up. When you hear the bell, it's time to swim.

Who's it for?
------------------------------

Everyone. You don't need to be an expert, and you don't need to have dropped a slip in the bowl. Jump in and talk if something sparks, or sit back and enjoy the show.

Drop in a hot take
------------------------------

Write a statement, not a question: something people can argue for or against. It can be about docs, tech, or work, and you don't even have to believe it. Defend it yourself on stage, or sit back and watch the other fish chew it over.

Need some inspiration? Hot takes look like this:

- "Nobody will ever RTFM."
- "AI is a flash in the pan, and my job is safe forever."
- "Tech writer recruitment processes are fun and easy."

Good to know
------------------------------

- **When**: Day 1, 14:00 to 14:45, straight after the lightning talks.
- **In person only**: The fishbowl isn't streamed. Online attendees can hang out in the online hallway track during this time.
- **Not recorded**: So you can speak candidly.
- **Code of conduct**: As always, our [code of conduct](/conf/{{shortcode}}/{{year}}/code-of-conduct) applies.
