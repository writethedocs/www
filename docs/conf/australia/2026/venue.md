# Venue

## Treasury Theatre

Lower Plaza, 1 Macarthur St, Melbourne

Our conference will be held at [Treasury Theatre](https://www.dtf.vic.gov.au/treasury-theatre-bookings), a modern theatre-style auditorium.

Established as the State Film Centre in the  1960s, the theatre underwent an architect-led refurbishment in 2015. Its state-of-the-art  audio visual systems and elegant heritage features are sure to impress.

The venue is conveniently located in central  Melbourne, near Treasury Gardens, on the corner of Spring Street and Macarthur Street in East Melbourne.

## Getting there

The Treasury Theatre is easily accessible by public transport. The venue is centrally located and well serviced by Melbourne's public transport system, including buses, trams, and a train station right opposite the venue.

Stairs lead from the Upper Plaza at street level of 1 Macarthur Street down to the theatre. It is also possible to access the theatre via Spring Street and Treasury Place. (See the accessible pathway map).

### Train

The theatre is conveniently located near both the Spring Street exit and Macarthur Street exit of Parliament Station.

### Trams

The free City Circle tram and tram routes 11, 12 and 109 stop at the intersection of Spring Street and Collins Street.

### Parking

On-street paid parking and paid car parks are available within walking distance, such as Wilson Parking at the Park Hyatt Hotel and also at Collins Place.

## Accessibility

### Audio Loop

The auditorium is equipped with audio loop technology, delivering crystal-clear sound directly to hearing aids and assistive listening devices.

### Wheelchairs

A dedicated bathroom is available. The auditorium has two dedicated spaces for wheelchairs. A wheelchair lift provides access to the auditorium. Lift platform dimensions: 110cm x 84cm.

An accessible pathway to the venue is available via Spring Street.
