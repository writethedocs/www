# {{name}} {{year}} Recap - Feedback, Photos, Upcoming Events

Hi everyone,

Thanks for joining us for this year's {{name}} conference. We had an excellent time, and hope you did too.
We're so grateful to our sponsors, speakers, volunteers, and attendees for making the conference possible!

We've included information about photos, upcoming events, and ways to stay connected with the community.
Once videos are ready we'll send another update, but we wanted to get this out to you as soon as possible.

## Tell us how we did

**Our [short feedback survey](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSexqSjZ4m2IMg5rPsYeJxsyBcnTJP8rBbUZ-1OwSQtsZ-xwrw/viewform) is quick and easy**, so please fill it out.

Your feedback helps us improve our other events, and we really appreciate everyone who fills it out each year. While we can't act on everything, the team reads every response and takes it on board for future events.

## Conference photos

The initial conference photos are now available in our [{{name}} {{year}} Flickr album](https://www.flickr.com/photos/writethedocs/albums/72177720335472747/). We try to publish some during the event, and then release them all after editing in 1-2 weeks.

You're welcome to use them for non-commercial purposes, such as conference write-ups, as per our [Creative Commons License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.0/).

## Conference talk videos and sketchnotes

**Talk videos and sketchnotes will be available in the next 1-2 weeks.** We are working to edit them and get them released as soon as possible.

## Join our Slack network

Our Slack network is a great way to stay connected to the community beyond our conferences. We have channels for specific doc tools, meetups, career advice, and other areas of discussion.

Check out the [Slack page](https://www.writethedocs.org/slack/) on our website for more information.

## Join a local meetup

Participating in your [local meetup](https://www.writethedocs.org/meetups/) is a great way to keep in touch with other documentarians. We have meetups in cities around the world.

## Come to our upcoming events

We have more conferences coming up. Keep an eye on our conference pages and newsletter for announcements and dates:

- [Australia 2026](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/australia/2026/), December 3-4, Melbourne, Australia. **Call for Papers about to close.**
- Portland 2027, May 2-4, Portland, Oregon

## Thanks again

The conference is possible only because folks like you make it a great experience. Thanks for making it another wonderful year. We hope to see you back again next year, or even sooner at one of our other events.

And thanks again to all our sponsors:
